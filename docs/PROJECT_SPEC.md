# FrameInGoa - Project Specification

## 1. Overview
FrameInGoa is a premium personalized digital frame generator for HH Goa 2026. It allows users to upload a photo, adjust it, and instantly receive a branded HH Goa 2026 graphic (either a PFP Frame or a Builder ID Card) ready to download and share on X.

## 2. Product Flow
The application follows a strictly defined, one-pass flow with no login or signup gates:
1. **Landing:** Premium, cinematic coastal/ocean video background with accessible overlays and smooth animations.
2. **Identity:** User fills in their dynamic inputs (Name, Role, Email).
3. **Frame Selection:** User selects one of the exactly two fixed frame templates:
   - SUNSET — Above the Horizon
   - WAVE — Into the Tide
4. **Photo Upload:** User uploads a raw photo (JPG, PNG, HEIC).
5. **Photo Adjustment:** User can zoom, pan, reset, and confirm the photo to fit perfectly into the standardized circular portrait area.
6. **Result:** Instant generation of the final graphic combining the artwork and the photo.
7. **Download / Share:** 
   - Download the high-res image.
   - Share to X with a pre-filled tweet and the `#FrameInGoa` hashtag.

## 3. Data & Dynamic Inputs
- **Identity Fields:** Name, Role, Email. Never hardcode these.
- **Photo:** User-uploaded portrait.
- **Builder ID:** Dynamically generated in the format `GOA-HH-[UNIQUE CODE]`.

## 4. Frame Artwork Rules
- The two poster assets (SUNSET and WAVE) are **fixed visual artwork**.
- They must **NOT** be regenerated by AI or modified by the application.
- The artwork must be maintained as a fixed layer, while the user's processed circular photo acts as a separate dynamic layer.
- **Critical Standardization:** SUNSET and WAVE must have exactly the same:
  - Aspect ratio
  - Poster container dimensions
  - Portrait circle diameter
  - Portrait positioning system
  - Identity text region
  - Output dimensions

## 5. Technical Requirements
- **Speed:** Instant generation; no long loading screens.
- **Responsiveness:** Fully mobile-friendly; handles different screen sizes elegantly.
- **Image Processing:** Robust handling of real photos (different crops and aspect ratios) directly in the browser.
- **Output:** A real, downloadable image file.
- **Social Sharing:** Proper handling of X share intent, ensuring the hashtag `#FrameInGoa` is included.
